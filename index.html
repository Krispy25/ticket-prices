How the value score works

Each listing gets a seat-quality score from 0 to 100, then is ranked against every other listing for the event, on all marketplaces.

  • Quality comes from the seating level (floor and lower bowl rank highest) and how close the row is to the front. Limited or obstructed views lose 20 points; front rows and aisles gain a little.
  • Price is the all-in price per ticket with fees, before tax, so every marketplace compares fairly.
  • Seats on the orange line are the safe picks: no other listing is both better and cheaper.
